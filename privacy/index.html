Privacy Policy for Indian Driving Simulator

Effective date: September 5, 2026

7 Stones Interactive (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Indian Driving Simulator mobile application (the “App”) handles information when you use it.

Information we collect

The App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, payment details, or advertising information.

The App uses Google Play Games Services to provide Google Play Games sign-in and a leaderboard. When you use these features, Google Play Games Services may process:

  • Your Google Play Games gamer identity, such as your gamertag and avatar;
  • Your game score submitted to the App’s leaderboard;
  • Device or other identifiers used to operate the service; and
  • Analytics and diagnostic information used to maintain and improve Google Play Games Services.

Google Play Games Services transmits this information securely using HTTPS. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy.

How information is used

Information processed through Google Play Games Services is used only to sign you in to Google Play Games, submit and display your leaderboard score, and operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Google Play Games service.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use it for marketing profiles.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers or other independent third parties. Google Play Games Services processes the information described above to provide the game-services functionality. Your leaderboard name and score may be visible to other players according to your Google Play Games profile and privacy settings.

Data retention and deletion

We do not operate a server or maintain a separate database of player information. You can manage or delete Google Play Games data through your Google Play Games profile or your Google Account. For questions about this policy, contact us at ps.prasanth.interactive@gmail.com.

Children

The App is not directed to children under the minimum age required to use Google Play Games Services in their country. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us at ps.prasanth.interactive@gmail.com.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s data practices change. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when we do.
